What changed from v1
  • BrandBlack and gold from the MP logo, with a Didone serif for headings. v1 used forest green, which isn't part of the brand.
  • PortfolioCovers everything Mstar sells: condos, houses, land plots, hotels for sale and new developments. Hotels and land get their own fact fields (rooms and occupancy for hotels, rai/ngan/wah and zoning for land).
  • ContactLINE comes first, then a phone call, then the enquiry form. On phones they stay pinned to the bottom of the screen.
  • Detail pageFollows the page order from your old site sequence: gallery with enquiry box, overview, description, details, floor plan, nearby, map, mortgage calculator, video tour, then the 3-step visit.
  • LanguageThai/English switch in the header. The Thai font is set from the start, so Thai text doesn't fall back to a system font.
  • ScopeThis covers the public pages only. Accounts, admin screens and backend planning come after you approve the layout.
Brand tokens

Mostly white pages with black type. Gold is kept for prices, active filters and small accents. Black is used for the header, footer and a couple of feature bands, so it feels premium without making every page dark.

  1. Photo grid: one large photo and four small ones. Clicking opens a full-screen gallery with arrows and a counter.

  9. The 3-step virtual visit, repeated here.
  10. Agent box that follows you down the page: LINE first, then call, then a short form. One clear button.
  11. Similar properties: same area, same type, similar price.
  12. Phone: one long page with sections that open and close. The LINE / Call / Enquire bar replaces the side box.

Questions for you

  1. What did you dislike most about the Codex version? The look (colours, layout), the page structure, or both? The answer decides how far v3 moves from this.
  2. Light or dark site? This draft uses white pages with black and gold accents. A fully black luxury site is also possible but harder to read for long listings.
  3. Which language opens first: Thai or English? Are most buyers Thai or foreign?
  4. Hotels and land: should they sit under "Investment" as shown, or be their own menu items?
  5. Exact address: show the pin on the map, or only the general area until a viewing is booked?
  6. Any sites you like? Send two or three links and I'll match the feel.
