Mstar Property · Home page · wireframe v3

Home page, full length

Every section of the home page on desktop and phone. Build to BUILD-SPEC.md section 5.1. Photos, listings and prices are placeholders.

Sections, top to bottom
  1. HHeader: logo only (no text). Buy · Rent · New projects · Investment · Services on one line. Language/currency, Saved, List your property, Sign in (rectangle).
  2. 1Hero: one large real photo, heading and one line of text. No slider.
  3. 2Search box: Buy / Rent / New projects / Investment tabs; Location · Type · Price · Beds · search. Phone: one "Where?" field and the button.
  4. 3Location suggestions (click Location): recent searches, areas and BTS/MRT, projects.
  5. 4Homes for you: listing carousel right under the search box. Tabs For sale / For rent / New listings / Price reduced / Investment, arrows, See all. 3 cards visible on desktop, 1 and a bit on phone (swipe). Card: 3:2 photo with badges, next-photo arrow, dots, heart bottom-right; status dot + type ("Condo for sale"); big price + green price drop; bold facts for its type; 2-line address; Contact agent button that opens the contact form. Try it.
  6. 5Category icons that scroll sideways. Each opens results with that filter on.
  7. 6Continue your search: shown only when this device has a recent or saved search or a viewed listing.
  8. 7Explore by location: Bangkok, Pattaya, Phuket, Rayong with live listing counts.
  9. 8New projects by Mstar: 3 project cards with a status badge. Phone: sideways carousel.
  10. 9Tools: mortgage calculator, what is my home worth, buying guide for foreigners.
  11. –Services for owners: management, cleaning, contractor, home decor. Then the light grey footer.
  12. TPhone only: bottom tab bar Explore · Search · Saved · LINE chat · Account.
